# Objectives

## 1. Primary Objective

To develop a real-time driver behaviour anomaly detection system using computer vision and deep learning techniques to identify potentially unsafe driver behaviours and provide timely alerts for improved road safety.

---

## 2. Specific Objectives

### 2.1 Driver Identification

To identify the driver using a face recognition module and associate the detected behaviour with the corresponding driver.

### 2.2 Facial Behaviour Monitoring

To monitor facial characteristics using facial landmark detection and extract relevant features such as:

- Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR)
- Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR)
- Head Pose
- Eye state
- Facial movement

These features are used to identify behavioural indicators such as eye closure, yawning, talking, and changes in head orientation.

### 2.3 Drowsiness and Fatigue Detection

To detect possible driver drowsiness by analysing eye closure, blink-related information, yawning, and other facial characteristics over a period of time.

The system should distinguish between short-duration normal actions, such as blinking, and prolonged patterns that may indicate fatigue.

### 2.4 Driver Distraction Detection

To identify potentially distracting driver behaviours by analysing facial/head orientation and relevant visual cues.

The system also considers distraction-related objects detected within the driver's environment.

### 2.5 Object Detection

To use YOLOv8 for detecting objects that may be associated with driver distraction, such as:

- Mobile phones
- Water bottles
- Cups
- Other relevant objects

### 2.6 Feature Extraction and Fusion

To convert the outputs of the individual monitoring modules into numerical feature representations.
