# Phase 1 Summary

## 1. Introduction

Phase 1 of the project focused on understanding the problem of driver behaviour anomaly detection, studying existing approaches, exploring suitable datasets, identifying research and technology gaps, and determining a suitable technical direction for further development.

The main objective of Phase 1 was not to complete the final system, but to establish the research foundation and baseline direction for Phase 2 implementation.

---

# 2. Project Background

Driver behaviour is an important factor in road safety. Unsafe behaviours such as drowsiness, distraction, inattention, and other abnormal driving patterns can increase the risk of accidents.

The project was initiated to investigate how computer vision and deep learning techniques can be used to monitor driver behaviour and provide timely alerts when potentially unsafe behaviour is detected.

The initial project direction focused broadly on:
